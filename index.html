I'm a cybersecurity major at Old Dominion University, focused on the intersection of technology, human behavior, and security policy.

My work explores how social science frameworks — psychology, sociology, economics, and ethics — shape the way organizations and individuals respond to cyber threats. I'm especially drawn to topics like social engineering, digital privacy, and the human factors that make security policies actually work (or quietly fall apart).

This portfolio collects the work I've completed during my studies in Cybersecurity and the Social Sciences — papers, reviews, presentations, and reflections that connect classroom theory to real-world cybersecurity challenges.
